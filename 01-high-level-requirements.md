# GoldPathArchery high-level requirements

Status: initial requirements outline for discussion.

## Scope and interpretation

This document describes what GoldPathArchery must provide at a high level. It does not select technologies or define implementation details. The areas below identify intended capabilities; release allocation, priorities, detailed rules, and acceptance criteria are TBD.

TBD means a decision has not yet been agreed. Future detail will be recorded in the [requirements index](requirements/README.md). Project direction is summarized in the [master plan](00-master-plan.md).

## 1. Purpose and vision

Provide a shared platform for archers and coaches to organize training, reflect on performance, and support progression toward athlete goals.

TBD: target audiences, supported archery disciplines, initial scope, and measures of success.

## 2. User types and roles

Support the needs of archers/athletes, coaches, administrators, and parents/guardians of youth athletes where applicable.

TBD: role definitions, permissions, eligibility, and whether one account may hold multiple roles.

## 3. Authentication and account access

Provide secure account access and appropriate account recovery and account lifecycle support.

TBD: registration, sign-in options, verification, recovery rules, account closure, and access restrictions.

## 4. Archer profiles

Provide an archer profile that supports the athlete's training and coaching context.

TBD: required and optional profile information, visibility, editing permissions, and discipline-specific needs.

## 5. Coach and athlete relationships

Allow coaches and athletes to establish and manage coaching relationships with appropriate access to shared information.

TBD: invitations, approval, relationship limits, shared information, and access when a relationship ends.

## 6. Training plans

Support the creation, assignment, and review of training plans for athletes.

TBD: plan ownership, structure, scheduling, changes, completion tracking, and sharing rules.

## 7. Warm-up and recovery plans

Support warm-up and recovery activities as part of an athlete's training preparation and follow-up.

TBD: plan content, authorship, personalization, and how these plans relate to training sessions.

## 8. Training journal

Allow athletes to record training activity and reflections and review their training history.

TBD: journal content, editing rules, visibility, and coach access.

## 9. Competition planning

Support athletes and coaches in planning for competitions and related preparation.

TBD: competition information, planning workflows, scheduling, and the scope of preparation support.

## 10. Performance and results tracking

Allow athletes to record and review performance and competition results over time.

TBD: supported result formats, metrics, comparisons, validation, and sharing permissions.

## 11. Goals and progression

Support setting athlete goals and reviewing progress toward them.

TBD: goal types, ownership, review periods, progression measures, and completion rules.

## 12. Coach feedback

Allow coaches to provide feedback to athletes in the context of their training and performance.

TBD: feedback formats, associated records, athlete responses, visibility, and history.

## 13. Video and technical analysis

Support the use of video and technical observations to assist athlete and coach review of archery technique.

TBD: submission, review, analysis capabilities, sharing permissions, and retention. Automated analysis scope is not yet agreed.

## 14. Notifications

Provide relevant notifications about training and coaching activity, with appropriate user controls.

TBD: notification events, recipients, delivery channels, preferences, and youth-related restrictions.

## 15. AI-assisted training capabilities

Provide AI-assisted support for training and reflection within an agreed scope and with appropriate human oversight.

TBD: supported use cases, boundaries, user controls, transparency, coach review, safety expectations, and use of athlete information. No autonomous coaching decisions or specific analysis capabilities are assumed by this outline.

## 16. Administration

Provide administration capabilities needed to manage accounts, access, and platform operations.

TBD: administrative responsibilities, permissions, support workflows, moderation needs, and accountability records.

## 17. Security and privacy

Protect accounts and athlete information and restrict access according to agreed permissions and sharing choices.

TBD: information classification, privacy defaults, access controls, retention, deletion, audit needs, and incident handling requirements.

## 18. Youth athlete and parent/guardian controls

Support age-appropriate participation and parent/guardian involvement where required, with controls over access and information sharing.

TBD: youth eligibility, age thresholds, guardian verification, consent, oversight, coach communications, and transition to adult account access. These decisions require safeguarding and jurisdiction-specific review.

## 19. GDPR requirements

Support compliance with applicable GDPR obligations for the personal information handled by the platform.

TBD: applicable jurisdictions, controller and processor responsibilities, lawful bases, privacy notices, data minimization, retention, data subject rights, consent where applicable, youth-specific obligations, international transfers, and breach response. Assessments and review responsibilities must be defined before requirements in this area are finalized; this outline is not a completed compliance assessment.

## 20. Non-functional requirements

Provide a usable, accessible, reliable platform with performance and capacity appropriate to its agreed audience and use cases.

TBD: measurable targets for accessibility, usability, performance, availability, capacity, recovery, compatibility, maintainability, and operational support. No numerical service targets are agreed yet.

## 21. Future capabilities

Maintain a place to record potential capabilities beyond the agreed release scope.

TBD: future capabilities and their priorities. No additional features are committed by this section; proposals will be evaluated and documented before entering delivery scope.

## Next requirements work

Expand each area only after its scope and key decisions are agreed. Detailed documents should capture workflows, permissions, open questions, and testable acceptance criteria, and link back to this outline.
