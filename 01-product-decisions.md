## Established direction

The product brief describes a fresh service for recurve archers and coaches, with potential parent support for juniors. The first release covers profiles, weekly plans, session logging, basic progress summaries and administration. Start with a free private pilot. Payments, competition registration, equipment sales, video analysis, social feeds, native apps and automatic AI coaching are outside the first release.
